Always-on display

Bright and dark keep warm yellow tones. After 30 seconds without activity, minimal mode uses pale yellow button outlines on dim, neutral panels. The clock colon stays steady and Dot becomes an outline portrait. Touch or move to wake. Drafts stay in place.

Full screen and screen wake lock depend on browser support. Minimal mode reduces the page’s brightness; device backlight settings remain separate.

Notification behavior

Arrange your panels

Long-press a panel for half a second to reveal its corner menu and enable dragging. Big spans two grid columns; Medium spans one. Auto uses the panel’s default grid size. Portrait has two columns and three visible rows; landscape has three columns and two visible rows. Pin locks the panel’s order. A keyboard user can focus a panel and press Enter for its menu. Spread or pinch two fingers on panel background to step through declared sizes. The menu also offers temporary Expand, Reduce and Restore. Temporary sizes do not replace your saved layout; displaced panels remain accessible by scrolling.
